Axendra Community Rules and Reporting Policy

Effective: August 30, 2026
English · 简体中文 · Terms · Privacy · Delete account

1. Purpose and scope

The Axendra community is for voluntarily shared, source-aware insights. These rules apply to every public contribution, report, and interaction in or through community features. Private Cognitive Map content is not community content unless you explicitly submit it.

2. What you may share

Share your own original insight, a concise commentary, or material you have the right to share. Include accurate source context when relevant. A source title, author, short quotation, or link does not give permission to reproduce substantial copyrighted text or another person's private material.

3. Prohibited content and conduct

  • Content that is illegal, threatening, harassing, hateful, sexually exploitative, violent, fraudulent, or intended to harm or intimidate.
  • Personal data, private communications, doxxing, impersonation, or content shared without the affected person's permission.
  • Copyrighted, trademarked, or confidential material you do not have the right to use, including extensive book text, annotations, or material copied from third-party services.
  • Spam, scams, deceptive promotion, malware, automated abuse, or attempts to bypass Axendra controls.
  • Misleading claims presented as professional legal, medical, financial, or mental-health advice, or content that encourages self-harm, dangerous conduct, or unlawful activity.

4. Review and enforcement

Axendra may review a submission before publication and may remove, limit, de-index, preserve for investigation, or decline to publish content that violates these rules, the Terms of Use, or law. We may warn, restrict, suspend, or permanently block accounts that violate these rules. Review does not guarantee that all problematic content will be detected.

5. Report content or conduct

To report public content or user conduct, email axendra.support@gmail.com with the subject “Axendra community report.” Include the content location or screenshot, the account name if visible, the reason for the report, and any information needed to understand the issue. Do not include more private information than necessary. We will review reports in good faith and may request additional information.

6. Intellectual-property complaints and appeals

For a copyright, trademark, or other intellectual-property complaint, use the subject “Axendra intellectual property complaint” and include the allegedly infringing content location, a description of your rights, your contact information, and a good-faith statement that the use is unauthorised. We may remove or restrict content during review. A contributor who believes moderation was incorrect may email axendra.support@gmail.com with the subject “Axendra moderation appeal,” identify the affected content, and explain the appeal.

7. Contact

Questions about these rules or moderation may be sent to axendra.support@gmail.com. See also the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

Axendra 社区规则与投诉处理政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 30 日

1. 目的与适用范围

Axendra 社区用于分享自愿公开、具有来源意识的认知。本规则适用于社区功能中的每一条公开贡献、举报及互动。除非你明确提交，私密认知图谱内容不属于社区内容。

2. 可以分享的内容

你可以分享自己的原创认知、简短评论或你有权分享的资料；在相关时请提供准确来源背景。来源标题、作者、短引用或链接，并不意味着你可以复制大段受版权保护的文字或他人的私密资料。

3. 禁止的内容与行为

  • 违法、威胁、骚扰、仇恨、性剥削、暴力、欺诈，或意图伤害、恐吓他人的内容。
  • 个人信息、私人通信、人肉搜索、冒充他人，或未经当事人许可分享的内容。
  • 你无权使用的受版权、商标或保密保护资料，包括大段书籍文字、批注或从第三方服务复制的资料。
  • 垃圾信息、诈骗、误导性推广、恶意软件、自动化滥用，或绕过 Axendra 控制的行为。
  • 冒充专业法律、医疗、金融或心理健康建议的误导性主张，或鼓励自残、危险行为或违法活动的内容。

4. 审核与处置

Axendra 可在发布前审核内容，也可移除、限制、取消索引、为调查保留或拒绝发布违反本规则、服务条款或法律的内容。我们可对违规账户进行警告、限制、暂停或永久封禁。审核不保证能够发现所有问题内容。

5. 举报内容或行为

如需举报公开内容或用户行为，请发送邮件至 axendra.support@gmail.com，主题为“Axendra community report”。请提供内容位置或截图、可见时的账户名称、举报原因，以及理解问题所需的信息。请勿提供超出必要范围的私人信息。我们会善意审核举报，并可能要求补充资料。

6. 知识产权投诉与申诉

如需提出版权、商标或其他知识产权投诉，请使用主题“Axendra intellectual property complaint”，并提供涉嫌侵权内容的位置、你的权利说明、联系信息及你善意相信该使用未经授权的声明。审核期间我们可能移除或限制内容。贡献者如认为审核有误，可发送邮件至 axendra.support@gmail.com，主题为“Axendra moderation appeal”，说明受影响内容与申诉理由。

7. 联系方式

如对本规则或审核有疑问，请联系 axendra.support@gmail.com。另请参阅服务条款与隐私政策。
