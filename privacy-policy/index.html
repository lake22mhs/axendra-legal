Axendra Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Axendra helps people organize books and turn reading into useful knowledge.

Information we collect

We collect account information, profile details you choose to provide, authentication data, and content you intentionally add or import so Axendra can provide its features.

How we use information

We use information to provide and secure Axendra, sync your account, process imports, generate requested AI summaries or insights, communicate important service information, and improve the product when you allow analytics.

Privacy boundaries

Private notes, highlights, reflections, and other private knowledge are not published to public discovery or public profiles. Private content is not used as analytics event properties.

Sharing and service providers

We use infrastructure and service providers, including Supabase and AI providers, to operate features you request. We do not sell personal information.

Analytics

Axendra may collect limited product-usage events to understand activation, retention, and feature performance. Analytics excludes private content and direct identifiers. You can opt out in Settings where available.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account from the in-app Settings screen. Account deletion removes your account and associated personal data, subject to limited records retained when legally required.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: axendra.support@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as Axendra develops. The latest version is posted on this page.
